# TCC Digital Accounts & Ownership Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist is the operational plan for separating The Cleaning Company Sverige AB (TCC) from Victor's personal accounts.

### Core rule

> If TCC pays for it, stores TCC data, executes TCC processes, or contains TCC intellectual property, the account or workspace should belong to TCC.

Victor and Kristin may remain administrators, but TCC should not depend on a single personal account for ownership, recovery, billing, or business continuity.

# 9. Source-of-truth classification

For every system, define whether it is:

- **Canonical source of truth**
- **Operational system**
- **Interface**
- **Temporary working space**
- **Archive**
- **Derived/reporting system**

Do not allow two systems to silently become authoritative for the same business rule.

Examples:

- GitHub → canonical business logic/documentation
- Connecteam → operational scheduling/execution
- Fortnox → accounting/financial records
- Supabase → defined operational state/data
- WordPress → public website interface
- Miro → workshop/visual working space unless explicitly promoted to canonical documentation

# 11. Completion definition

This separation project is considered complete when:

- [ ] No critical TCC system depends solely on Victor's personal identity
- [ ] TCC owns its critical accounts, domains, data and intellectual property
- [ ] Billing is separated from personal expenses where appropriate
- [ ] Recovery and 2FA are documented
- [ ] At least two authorized people can recover critical systems where appropriate
- [ ] API keys and secrets are inventoried and controlled
- [ ] Every major system has a defined role
- [ ] Source-of-truth responsibility is explicit
- [ ] TCC can continue operating if Victor temporarily loses access to his personal devices/accounts

---

## Operating principle

**Same computer is acceptable. Shared ownership is not the goal; clear company ownership is.**

**TCC owns the assets. People receive authorized access.**
